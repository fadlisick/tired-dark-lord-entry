The lich and the teapot: a 100-word villain monologue from a dark lord who is secretly just very tired. His phylactery is a chipped teapot, his scheme is immortality decanted slowly, and his evenings all taste the same. He never says he is weary — the cold tea, the lost count of springs, and the trailing voice say it for him.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (`/output` by default), verifies the 1–600 character gate, and reads the file back to prove it matches.
